    Why this is one system, not two. The rig draws against a tiny Surface
    interface — ellipse, arc, fill,
    clip. A Canvas backend forwards those to the GPU; an SVG backend
    turns them into path data. Neither knows about the character. Add an
    expression once and it appears at runtime, in exported SVG, in the sprite
    sheet, and in the GIF.
